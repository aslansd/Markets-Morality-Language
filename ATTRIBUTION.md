# Sources and licences of bundled data

* **D-PLACE** (Kirby et al. 2016; CLDF release, https://github.com/D-PLACE/dplace-cldf) — CC BY 4.0. Subset of codes from the
  Ethnographic Atlas (Murdock), Standard Cross-Cultural Sample (Murdock & White; Barry et al. 1976; and others),
  Carneiro, and Binford (2001). File: `data/raw/dplace_data_subset.csv`, `societies.csv`.
* **Glottolog** (Hammarström, Forkel, Haspelmath & Bank; https://github.com/glottolog/glottolog-cldf) — CC BY 4.0.
  Files: `languages.csv`, `glottolog_values_subset.csv` (classification paths, endangerment status).
* **eBible corpus** (Akerman et al. 2023, https://github.com/BibleNLP/ebible) — metadata `translations.csv`, `vref.txt`.
  Only translations marked *Redistributable* are included, as New-Testament-only extracts, under their original
  licences (mostly CC BY-SA / CC BY-NC-ND / public domain; see eBible.org). Listed below.
* **eMFD** extended Moral Foundations Dictionary (Hopp, Fisher, Cornell, Huskey & Weber 2021,
  https://github.com/medianeuroscience/emfd).

Papers combined: Enke (2023) Nat Hum Behav 7:134–141, doi:10.1038/s41562-022-01480-x;
Koplenig et al. (2025) PLOS Complex Systems 2(2):e0000032, doi:10.1371/journal.pcsy.0000032.
